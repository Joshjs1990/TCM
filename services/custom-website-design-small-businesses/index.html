Custom Website Design for Small Businesses

I specialize in custom website design for small businesses that want to stand out online. Your website should feel like you — personal, functional, and built to connect with your audience.

Before I start designing, I take the time to understand your brand, values, and goals. That way, every page feels intentional and meaningful.

Why a Custom Website Is Worth It

A well-designed website does more than just look good — it helps build trust, convert visitors, and reflect your brand’s identity. While templates may seem quick and easy, they often fall short when it comes to flexibility, performance, or SEO.

Instead, I focus on building websites that are strategic, scalable, and future-ready. As a result, your site can grow with your business — not hold it back.

  • Custom Layout and Design Tailored to Your Brand

    Rather than using templates, I design your website from scratch. This ensures it matches your visual identity — colors, fonts, photos, tone — and resonates with your audience. Consequently, your site will feel aligned and professional from the very first click.

  • Mobile-Friendly and Responsive Build

    With so many users browsing on phones and tablets, mobile responsiveness is no longer optional. That’s why I design for all screen sizes from the start. Regardless of device, your visitors will enjoy a smooth, consistent experience.

  • SEO-Optimized Structure

    Great design should also perform. I build with SEO best practices in mind — from optimized headings and fast-loading pages to alt tags and metadata. As a result, your site will be easier to find on search engines.

  • Contact and Inquiry Forms

    Whether you want more leads, bookings, or questions, your site will include an easy-to-use contact form. These forms are secure, spam-protected, and send inquiries straight to your inbox — so you can respond quickly and confidently.

Unlike large agencies, I work one-on-one with small business owners, entrepreneurs, and creatives. 

This means no middlemen, no delays, and no generic results. From our first call to launch day, you’ll always know what’s happening. 

Moreover, you’ll get personal attention, clear communication, and a website that feels like it was made just for you.

Frequently Asked Questions.

How long does a website take to build?

Most small business websites take between 2–4 weeks from start to finish, depending on the number of pages and how quickly you can provide content and feedback. I’ll give you access to the site as soon as the homepage is looking good so we can work through together. I don’t agree on going away for a few weeks and then saying “here’s your site”. I believe more in collaboration.

Do you only design websites for small businesses?

While I specialize in website design and development for small businesses, I also work with entrepreneurs, startups, and creatives of all kinds. If you need a custom website that’s thoughtfully designed and built to perform, I’d love to work with you.

Will my website be mobile-friendly?

Absolutely. Every site I build is fully responsive, meaning it will look and work great on phones, tablets, and desktops. Mobile optimization is a core part of my design process.

Can I update the website myself after it’s launched?

Yes! I build all websites using WordPress, and I’ll make sure you’re comfortable making updates. I also offer tutorials or ongoing support if you ever need help.

Do you offer SEO services?

I include basic SEO optimization in every build — like clean code, fast load times, optimized headings, and metadata. If you’re looking for ongoing SEO services (like blog strategy or backlinking), I also have an optional monthly service.

How much does a website cost?

Pricing depends on the scope of your project — like how many pages you need and whether you want add-ons like ecommerce or blog functionality. I’ll always give you a clear, upfront quote with no surprises. Affordable website design is one of my core values. Contact me to discuss.
