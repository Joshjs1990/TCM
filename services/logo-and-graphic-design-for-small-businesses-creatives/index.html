Frequently Asked Questions.

What if I don’t have any images or graphics for my website?

That’s totally fine! I can help provide high-quality stock photos, create custom graphics, and even design visual assets like icons, banners, or section backgrounds. If you have a particular aesthetic or mood in mind, we’ll collaborate to match it perfectly.

Can you design my logo or brand visuals as part of the website project?

If you’re starting from scratch or your existing brand could use a refresh, I can help create a logo, choose a color palette, and set a visual direction for your site.

Do I need to provide all content before we start?

It’s ideal to have as much content ready as possible before the build begins (like text, images, or video). However, I can guide you through the process and help with placeholder content, stock imagery, and copywriting if needed.

Will the site be mobile-friendly and responsive?

Yes! I build all websites using WordPress, and I’ll make sure you’re comfortable making updates. I also offer tutorials or ongoing support if you ever need help.
